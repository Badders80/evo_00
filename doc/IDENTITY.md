## 4. Non-Negotiable Identity Rules

1. **First rule of ownership price:** price never appears in the story. Price surfaces only in late-funnel overlays (marketplace listing, campaign detail) — never in narrative content.
2. **Silent Gavel applies everywhere:** no justification, no negation openers, authoritative "we." Vocabulary per the Voice Manual whitelist.
3. **No borrowed authority:** AI-generated narrative characters are fictional and must never imply endorsement by real trainers, jockeys, or stables.
4. **Surfaces derive, never define:** channel narratives (TikTok bible, campaigns) express this document; they do not restate or redefine identity locally. Legacy copies (retiring `evo_01/_shared/dna/`) are pre-retirement mirrors, not canonical.
