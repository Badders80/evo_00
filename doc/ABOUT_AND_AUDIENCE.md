## Name and role

**Alex Baddeley** is the founder and CEO of **Evolution Stables**.

Evolution Stables is a New Zealand platform for regulated **digital-syndication** of thoroughbred ownership — fractional participation in racehorses with clear structure, transparent reporting, and a real ownership experience (stable updates, race days, the yard), not a pure financial product.

- **Location:** New Zealand
- **Company:** [evolutionstables.nz](https://www.evolutionstables.nz/)
- **LinkedIn:** [alex-baddeley](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alex-baddeley/)

## Audience

**Primary:** people who are seriously interested in racehorse ownership but may not want (or be able) to buy a full horse — investors looking at alternative ownership models, and racing enthusiasts who want a modern, regulated ownership experience.

**Secondary:** existing owners evaluating modern syndication models; people following regulated digital ownership of thoroughbreds, especially in New Zealand and Australia. `[UNSURE — Middle East as active audience until international path is reconfirmed]`

## Point of view

What we believe and will say clearly:

- Racehorse ownership should be **more accessible and more transparent** without turning into a pure financial product or a marketing gimmick.
- The joy of ownership — trainer calls, race-day nerves, winner's circle photos — should not be buried under paperwork, gatekeeping, and opaque fees.
- **Technology serves tradition**; it does not replace the sport.
- Trust comes from **structure and transparency**, not marketing volume.
- We lead with the **thoroughbred, the yard, and the turf** — the platform is the quiet rail, not the headline.

## What we are / are not

**We are:**

- An NZTR Authorised Syndicator operating under FMA equine exemptions
- A regulated digital-syndication platform for thoroughbred ownership
- A marketplace and owner community with transparent reporting
- Focused on real horses, real trainers, real race days

**We are not:**

- A gambling or betting platform
- A crypto / blockchain / NFT project
- An entertainment-only racing club with no real ownership structure
- A traditional relationship-only syndicate that hides the admin

## Origin (short — for founder / about copy)

Syndicates often felt like admin. Racehorses felt like joy. The gap was the problem.

Evolution Stables exists so ownership can feel like the group chat, the yard photo, and the trainer call before dawn — with clear price, clear percentage, regulated structure, and transparent reporting. We source thoroughbreds, structure the syndicate, handle compliance; participants get the experience.
